# NAVIQRA — 15-day global acquisition-interest campaign

**Locked choices:** Apache-2.0; full-company acquisition preferred; global launch.  
**Campaign owner:** Founder. **Primary objective:** earn qualified strategic conversations and technical diligence, not manufacture an offer.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Do not claim Airbnb, Expedia, Booking Holdings, Amadeus, or Sabre is a customer, partner, investor, or acquirer without written evidence.
- Do not call synthetic sandbox inventory live, bookable, or supplier-integrated.
- Publish benchmark methodology and machine details. Label local microbenchmarks accurately.
- Ask for a technical evaluation or design-partner discussion first. Raise acquisition only after demonstrated strategic fit.
- Use public business contact channels and personalized outreach; no scraped personal addresses or bulk spam.

## Daily operating targets (targets, not claims)
- 1 useful public artifact or product improvement per day.
- 5–8 thoughtful, individually researched outreaches per day after the technical launch.
- 3 design-partner conversations; 2 technical evaluations; 1 external adapter contribution are stretch goals.
- Track qualified replies, technical reviews, integration attempts, pilot commitments, and diligence requests—not impressions alone.

## Campaign scorecard
Record date, company, contact role, public source for relevance, message version, status, next action, and evidence. Do not store personal sensitive information. A “qualified conversation” means a relevant decision-maker or technical evaluator has replied and agreed to a substantive discussion.
