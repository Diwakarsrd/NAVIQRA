# Acquisition and technical due diligence checklist

## Product and engineering
- [ ] Demonstrate all five core assets in a deployed environment.
- [ ] Run independent benchmark review with disclosed methodology and failures.
- [ ] Complete architecture, threat-model and code-quality review.
- [ ] Replace SQLite with managed PostgreSQL and ship migrations/backups.
- [ ] Integrate a production identity provider and role-based authorization.
- [ ] Add rate limits, audit logs, tracing, alerts, SLOs and disaster recovery.
- [ ] Validate provider contracts, sandbox/live separation, idempotency and refunds.
- [ ] Complete mobile authentication, device testing, accessibility and store compliance.

## IP and legal
- [ ] Confirm founder and contractor IP assignments.
- [ ] Review every dependency and its license; produce an SBOM.
- [ ] Establish contributor license agreement policy if required.
- [ ] Review trademarks and third-party brand usage.
- [ ] Document data provenance, consent, retention and deletion.
- [ ] Prepare privacy notice, terms, DPAs and security policies.

## Commercial
- [ ] Obtain 3–5 design-partner interviews and at least one signed pilot.
- [ ] Convert pilots into paid deployments.
- [ ] Measure ROI against buyer's existing baseline.
- [ ] Document pipeline, pricing, retention and support costs.
- [ ] Prepare customer references and product roadmap.

## Transaction readiness
- [ ] Cap table, incorporation records and tax filings.
- [ ] IP schedule and open-source obligations.
- [ ] Contracts, liabilities, employee/contractor agreements.
- [ ] Financial statements, revenue quality and customer concentration.
- [ ] Data room and management presentation.

NAVIQRA v0.3.0 is an engineering foundation, not completion of this checklist.
