# NAVIQRA: Strategic Technology Thesis

## Positioning
NAVIQRA is an open, embeddable infrastructure layer for specialized travel agents. The product combines:
1. Travel Agent Runtime: bounded task registry, execution records and explicit adapter boundary.
2. Five-tier traveler memory: profile, episodic, semantic, procedural and active context.
3. Travel Intelligence Graph: tenant-scoped nodes and typed relationships.
4. Group Consensus Engine: weighted member preferences and ranked candidate outcomes.
5. Travel Recovery: disruption cases and alternatives that require human selection.
6. Naviqra SDK: lightweight Python client for embedding the v2 API.

## Why this could matter to strategic buyers
A travel marketplace may value capabilities that improve discovery, group decision-making, post-booking servicing, and integration speed. NAVIQRA is designed to be evaluated as an infrastructure component, not only as a consumer application.

## What must be proven
- Independent benchmark suite with published task definitions and failure cases.
- Provider integrations with documented rights, sandbox evidence and live transaction boundaries.
- Security review covering tenant isolation, authentication, authorization, secrets, PII and audit logs.
- External developer adoption and design-partner deployments.
- Measurable improvement against each partner's baseline: conversion, service resolution, time-to-plan, recovery success and cost per completed task.
- IP provenance, contributor license agreements where appropriate, dependency/license inventory and data-processing agreements.

## Claims we do not make
This repository does not include production supplier inventory, live flight/hotel booking, a hosted multi-region service, SOC 2 certification, or a production-grade identity provider. Demo inventory is not bookable. Human approval is required before consequential booking execution. Acquisition, valuation and strategic interest are not guaranteed.
